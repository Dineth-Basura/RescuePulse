Help center

Manage reports and keep your response team informed.

Report an incident

Open the incident board and select Report incident. Enter the location, type, priority and a clear description. Your report starts with an Open status. Coordinators can assign it to a registered responder and set a response deadline.

Track the response

Select View on an incident to see its details, response notes and activity history. The reporter, assigned responder and coordinators can add notes. The assigned responder or a coordinator can update the status and incident details.

Open means the report needs attention. Acknowledged means someone has reviewed it. Resolved means the response work is complete. Coordinators can archive resolved incidents or restore them when follow-up is needed.

Find and share reports

Search by location, description, responder or incident reference. Combine status, priority and ownership filters to focus on your work. Use the archive selector to review completed records. Export CSV downloads all reports matching your current filters, including records on other pages.

Workspace access

Responders see reports they created or were assigned. Coordinators see all incident reports and manage assignments, deadlines, archived records and workspace notifications. Administrators use a separate control center for accounts, incident allocation, workload review, broadcasts and workspace policies. Their dashboard does not include weather assessment or report creation.

Risk assessments

Enter observed rainfall, wind speed, temperature, humidity and soil saturation for a district. The risk index runs from 0 to 100: below 40 is Low, 40 to 64 is Moderate, and 65 or above is High. Save an incident from the result if it needs follow-up.

How the index is calculated

The index uses a weighted heuristic: rainfall 35%, wind 25%, temperature 15%, humidity 10% and soil saturation 15%. It is decision support from manually entered observations, not a validated forecast or official emergency warning. Verify conditions before taking action.

Administrator controls

Administrators can create accounts, change roles, suspend or reactivate access, and end another account's sessions. Suspended accounts cannot sign in, but their existing reports and assignments are kept for review. Your own account and the primary administrator are protected against suspension and role changes.

Use Incident oversight to assign responders, set response deadlines, change priority and status, or archive resolved reports. Bulk operations apply to selected reports, with failures reported individually. The workload view highlights unresolved assignments, including work held by suspended accounts.

Workspace settings control public registration, Gemini availability and hourly AI request limits. Changes take effect without a restart. The audit trail records account and policy actions alongside incident activity. Administrators can send in-app broadcasts and export the queue or audit view as CSV.

AI assistance

Use Explain assessment after saving weather observations, or open an incident and select Generate briefing. Gemini can summarize the supplied information, suggest coordination steps and identify details to verify. You can copy a handover into the response note form, review and edit it, then post it yourself.

Selected observations or report details and up to five recent notes are sent to Google Gemini only when you request assistance. Avoid sensitive personal details. AI suggestions do not change incident records or contact emergency services, and may be inaccurate. Request limits and provider quotas apply.

Notifications and account settings

The notification feed contains updates within RescuePulse. Mark a notification as read to clear it from your unread count. RescuePulse does not send SMS messages or contact emergency services.

Open Account to change your password. A password change signs out all your sessions, so you will need to sign in again. Your account role determines which workspace actions are available.
