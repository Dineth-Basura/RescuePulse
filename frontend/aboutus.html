ABOUT RESCUEPULSE

One workspace for coordinated response

Keep observations, incident reports and response updates connected.

A clear view of the work

RescuePulse helps responders report what they see, while coordinators review priorities, assign work and track progress. Every report has a shared history so the next person can pick up where the team left off.

From observation to action

Record weather readings, review a district risk index and create an incident when conditions need follow-up. Assign a responder, set a deadline and record field updates until the incident is resolved.

Information that stays with the team

Search and export reports, review archived incidents and use workspace notifications to share updates. Role-based permissions keep coordination actions with the right people.
